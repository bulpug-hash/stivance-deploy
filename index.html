Frequently asked questions

What guests ask us most.

This website won't let me book my dates — what do I do?

Online availability typically opens about 12 months ahead, so dates further in the future may look unavailable even when we still have space. Send your dates through this page or message Dana on WhatsApp (+420 604 294 777) and we will confirm directly — direct guests usually get a small discount.

How do I pay the deposit and the rest?

We ask for a 50 percent deposit to confirm the booking — by Revolut QR code or bank transfer, whichever is easier for you. The remaining 50 percent is paid in cash on arrival (USD or LKR). The resort doesn't have a card terminal.

We are a family of four — which villa fits?

Each two-bedroom villa or apartment sleeps four (two adults plus two children, or four adults). For five or six people we usually pair two villas next to each other so the families share a terrace. Tell us how you'd like to split the group and we'll suggest the layout.

Can you send more photos of a specific villa?

Yes. We have additional photos for each villa and apartment beyond what is shown online. Mention the category in your enquiry (Superior Villa, Villa with Sea View, Beach Front Villa, Apartment) and Dana will send them by email or WhatsApp.

Can you arrange an airport transfer?

Yes. Most guests fly into Bandaranaike Intl. Airport (BIA) in Colombo — about a 6-hour drive to the resort. Send us your flight number and arrival time and we'll arrange a fixed-price transfer.

Are there local tours and excursions?

Yes. The most popular ones are Pigeon Island snorkelling, Koneswaram Temple in Trincomalee, and inland day trips to Sigiriya. We can book a driver-guide directly for you on the day.

Can you accommodate a special diet at breakfast?

Continental, vegetarian, Asian and American breakfast options are part of the regular menu. For specific diets like gluten-free, dairy-free or child-friendly portions, mention it in advance and the kitchen will prepare around it.

When is the best time to visit Trincomalee?

May to September is peak — driest weather, calmest sea. Late November to April is still warm but with more variable conditions. October to early November is the most uncertain period (transition between monsoons). The east coast pattern is roughly the inverse of the south and west coast.

Is the resort family- and child-friendly?

Yes — the larger villa sleeps up to 4 adults + 3 children. Cot can be arranged on request. Children's portions on the menu, soft sandy beach access, no street traffic on the property.

What language does the staff speak?

English (good), Sinhala/Tamil (native). The owner Dana is Czech-speaking — direct communication in Czech is welcome.

Are pets allowed?

No — for the comfort of all guests, pets are not permitted on the property.

Wi-Fi, power outlets and connectivity?

Free Wi-Fi in all villas. Sri Lanka uses Type D, M and G plug shapes — bring an adapter (or borrow from reception). Voltage 230 V.

Can I arrive late at night?

Standard check-in is 14:00 onwards. Late arrivals up to midnight are no problem — let us know your flight number and we'll keep your villa ready.

What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 14 days before check-in for direct enquiries. Partial refund up to 7 days. Within 7 days, the first night is non-refundable.
